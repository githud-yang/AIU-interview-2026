Subject: Scope enquiry: deadline-aware input-resolution selection for real-time object detection

Dear Editorial Office,

Could you please advise whether your journal accepts presubmission scope enquiries, and, if so, which editorial channel is appropriate?

We are developing a study of input-resolution selection for YOLO-based detection under time-varying computational load and application deadlines. The intended question is whether a selection strategy can reduce deadline misses while retaining detection quality relative to fixed-resolution and existing adaptive baselines.

Our current work is at the feasibility stage. We have a local measurement pipeline; we have not yet established a novel method, independent deployment results, or a completed study suitable for submission. We plan to evaluate end-to-end latency distributions, deadline misses, detection quality, and the overhead of the selection policy, while explicitly addressing related adaptive and streaming-detection work.

If scope enquiries are accepted, would a study addressing these issues be within the journal's remit? We understand that any scope guidance would not imply acceptance and that scientific assessment requires the completed manuscript and peer review.
